# Opening a business? Let a smart partner help you choose the right location.

Starting a business is a brave decision. But choosing the right location? That’s often the hardest part.

You’ve probably heard this:

“This place feels right.”
“There are lots of people here.”
“The rent is cheaper here.”

But feeling isn’t enough. And the wrong location can slow down even the best idea.

That’s exactly why Spotonaut was created.

Spotonaut is your smart partner for choosing a business location. We’re not corporate analytics software. We’re not complex tools for data scientists. Spotonaut is a simple assistant that shows you within minutes:

- How a location performs
- Location score (0–100)
- Foot traffic score
- Context of the surrounding area and competition

So you don’t make decisions based on intuition alone.

## Who is Spotonaut for?

For people who:

- are opening their first location
- are expanding to a second or third branch
- want to verify a specific address
- don’t want to take unnecessary risks

We don’t tell you what to do. We give you data to make better decisions.

A decision worth thousands shouldn’t be random. Rent. Renovation. Equipment. Marketing. Opening a new location is a serious investment. It should be based on more than just a feeling.

Spotonaut helps you see what’s not obvious at first glance.
